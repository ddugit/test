Sympto care is a health guidance project that helps people make safer decisions for common illnesses using Traditional Indian Medical Systems such as **Ayurveda, Unani, Siddha, and Sowa-Rigpa**.  
It focuses on reducing harmful self-medication and reviving forgotten preventive healthcare knowledge.

This project does NOT replace doctors.  
It guides users toward responsible care and warns against careless use of strong chemical medicines.

## Problem We Are Solving

People commonly self-medicate for:
- Fever  
- Cold and cough  
- Headache  
- Stomach pain  
- Acidity  
- Body pain  
- Allergies  
- Minor infections  

They often use:
- Antibiotics  
- Strong painkillers  
- Steroids  
- Combination syrups  

These drugs are high-prescription medicines and unsafe when used without supervision. Over time, this causes:
- Liver and kidney damage  
- Antibiotic resistance  
- Weak immunity  
- Masking of serious diseases  

At the same time, India’s traditional medicine systems are slowly disappearing from daily use.

---

## Our Solution

Sympto Caare provides:
- Traditional preventive guidance for mild conditions  
- Herbal and lifestyle suggestions  
- Diet recommendations  
- Clear warnings about unsafe medicine use  
- Advice on when doctor consultation is necessary
